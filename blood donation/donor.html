Why Become a Donor?

Blood donation is a simple, yet powerful act that can save lives. By becoming a donor, you contribute to the well-being of your community and help those in need during emergencies.

  • Save Lives: Each donation can save up to three lives.
  • Health Benefits: Regular donation can improve cardiovascular health.
  • Community Impact: Help maintain a stable blood supply for hospitals.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Age: 18-65 years
  • Weight: Minimum 50 kg
  • Health: Must be in good health and free from infections
  • Donation Frequency: At least 3 months since last donation
